Subject: Suggestion – Evaluating an AI Command Centre for the Organisation

Overview

An AI Command Centre can serve as a unified control plane for discovering, governing, and orchestrating AI usage across the organisation. Before investing, I recommend focusing on platforms that deliver genuine agentic coordination and risk intelligence rather than dashboard aesthetics.

Key Considerations

1. Capability Fit
   - Does the platform provide AI discovery across endpoints, servers, and cloud services?
   - Does it offer correlated risk intelligence with automated response workflows?
   - Can it orchestrate and monitor multiple AI agents under a unified governance policy?

2. Security & Compliance
   - Ensure the solution supports data sovereignty, auditability, and role-based access controls.
   - Verify that AI model and data usage remains within organisational boundaries.

3. Integration
   - The platform should integrate with existing security, observability, and workflow tools without requiring a full stack replacement.

4. ROI Clarity
   - Look for measurable outcomes: reduction in shadow AI, faster incident response, improved compliance posture.

Recommendation

Pilot a solution that emphasises autonomous, policy-driven response (e.g., agentic triage and remediation) over passive monitoring. Start with a bounded scope—such as SaaS AI usage and endpoint agent discovery—and expand based on demonstrated value.

Next Steps

- shortlist 2–3 vendors with proven enterprise deployments
- run a 30-day pilot focused on AI discovery and risk correlation
- define success metrics before the pilot begins
